Privacy Policy for DeutschCards

Last updated: 7 November 2026

DeutschCards is a German flashcard app published by Muhammad Qasim. This

policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version:

it collects nothing and sends nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. DeutschCards does not collect, transmit, sell or share any

personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no

analytics.

The app requests no Android permissions at all — not even internet access.

You can verify this yourself: Android Settings → Apps → DeutschCards →

Permissions. Without the INTERNET permission the operating system will not

let the app contact a server, so it could not send your data anywhere even if

it were asked to.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps two things in its own private storage:

  1. Deck content — the German vocabulary that ships inside the app.
  2. Your study progress — which cards you have seen, how you rated them,

when each is next due, and your settings.

Both stay in the app's private sandbox. No other app can read them. Deleting

the app deletes both.

Android backup

If you have Android's backup to Google Drive switched on, Android may include

your study progress in your device backup, so a new phone restores your

streak. This is handled entirely by Android and your Google account, not by

DeutschCards, and it is governed by

Google's privacy policy.

You can turn it off in Android Settings → Google → Backup, or for this app

specifically through your device's backup settings. The bundled deck content

is explicitly excluded from backup.

Text-to-speech

German pronunciation is produced by the text-to-speech engine already

installed on your device. The app hands it the word to pronounce and the

engine speaks it. DeutschCards bundles no audio recordings and does not

record anything through your microphone — it has no microphone permission.

Depending on which engine you use and how you have configured it, your

device's text-to-speech may process text on-device or through its own

provider. That behaviour belongs to the engine you have installed (commonly

Google Speech Services) and its own privacy policy, not to this app.

Children

DeutschCards is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it

collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the date

at the top updated.
